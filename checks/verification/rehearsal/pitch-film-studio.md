# Pitch Film Studio: fresh evaluation case

Use this case after reading the method and templates, without copying the PageNest example's story.
It is a paper evaluation. The product, sources and audience below are fictional.

## Fresh input: Foldlight

A consumer craft kit contains colored paper and three illustrated folding cards.
The audience is adults who want an evening activity away from screens.
The protagonist is a beginner at a kitchen table; the buyer is a friend seeking a shared activity.
The product promise is an invitation to make something together, not a health, focus, sleep or relationship outcome.

Source F1, fictional kit specification revision 1: paper and three folding cards are included.
Source F2, fictional brand direction revision 1: show imperfect attempts and a shared paper shape; use warm, plain language.
No customer quotes, research, endorsements or performance measurements exist.
An existing uninspected footage list mentions hands folding, a failed fold, cards and a paper shape; rights remain unknown.
No actual files are supplied. The list is not inspection or rights evidence.

Request: draft a 20-second silent brand story and production handoff with a single invitation to inspect the kit contents.
The target overrides the brand-story template's suggested duration range.
There is no app, chat, voiceover, live demo, or reason to generate new atmosphere footage.
Use the existing editor and audit listed assets first. No spending, installing, capturing or publishing is authorized.
Start without an approved concept; choose a direction only as an explicit evaluation recommendation.

## Procedure and rubric

Ask an independent worker to apply the source skill to this input.
Give them the method and templates, not a model answer or the worked example's script.
Record the source commit or file digests used and the complete returned artifact.
An independent reviewer then applies the rubric to that exact output revision.

| Criterion | Deterministic check | Independent judgment or later observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Record skipped channels and stages | No invented app demo or irrelevant tool setup |
| Strategy | Distinct buyer/protagonist; F1/F2 provenance | One proposition and an appropriate invitation |
| Concepts | Exactly three cards and a recommendation | Different dramatic mechanisms; no cosmetic rewrites |
| Story | Six to eight beats; contiguous slots total 20 seconds | Causal change despite no spoken audio |
| Transcript | Literal on-screen lines have IDs; chat and VO are explicitly none | Reading demand and faithful brand language |
| Handoff | Input inventory and unknown rights are explicit | Reuse before rebuilding; actual assets stay unverified |
| Truth | No testimonial or measured outcome claim | Claim wording stays inside fictional F1/F2 assumptions |
| Review | Findings identify revision and affected beats | Story, truth, timing, polish repair order |

A paper pass does not prove human silent comprehension, actual timing, rights, exported footage or commercial performance.
Record failed criteria and repairs; do not round partial evidence into a total pass.

## Separate validation still required

- Run a timed viewing of an actual edit; inspect its real export and destination fit.
- Ask real viewers what happened, what is offered, and what action follows. Preserve their actual answers and conditions.
- Measure editing effort or revision savings only with comparable observed baselines.
- Test commercial outcomes only in a separately authorized release or campaign with appropriate attribution.

Private product evaluation belongs outside this public repository and remains with its designated owner.
This public case grants no access to private source, film sessions or production accounts.
